Broadband That Goes Where You Go

A MiFi is a compact portable router that connects to the mobile network and creates a Wi-Fi connection for your devices. It allows you to connect laptops, tablets, phones and other devices without needing a traditional fixed broadband connection.
Whether you’re away from the office, working at a temporary location or travelling for business, a MiFi gives you a simple and flexible way to access the internet wherever suitable mobile coverage is available.
